ELUXIOR Photo Receipt Control

The previous photo-review interface has been retired. There is no manual or automatic product-image approval stage.

After an authorized vendor uploads a technically valid image to the correct Purchase Order and SKU and the system stores its receipt, the vendor receives Upload confirmed.

The vendor remains responsible for the photographed product matching the ordered item. Receipt of an image does not mean ELUXIOR or TJX has approved the product.

Internal receipt tracking and any PO/SKU corrections must use the governed ELUXIOR Photo Card Control process. This page does not collect credentials or make changes.
