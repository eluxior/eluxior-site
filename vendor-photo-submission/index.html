Vendor instructions · New procedure from 01 November 2026

Submit your PO product photos

This procedure is mandatory for every new ELUXIOR Purchase Order. It takes only four steps. You must send at least one clear reference photograph for every distinct Style Number on each PO.

One PO = one PO Photo Card file. Open the PO link in your Digest A or B, or enter the PO number below. Verify your authorized email in the secure portal, then check the vendor, PO and SKU list and download the PO Photo Card. For each product, write only the STYLE NUMBER and PHOTO DATE. The same PO card is reused for each SKU in that PO.

1Download

Open the PO link or enter its number below. Verify your email, then check the vendor, PO and SKU list before downloading the card. If the PO is unavailable, contact ELUXIOR.

2Write

Write the exact STYLE NUMBER and DATE OF PHOTO clearly and in large characters on the printed card. Nothing else is needed.

3Photograph

Photograph the complete product with the whole card visible and a measuring tape beside the product. The written Style Number and date must be legible. One photo per distinct SKU per PO; different sizes/colours/finishes require separate photos. Keep each photo under 2 MB without sacrificing readability.

4Upload

Use the Upload photographs button on that PO's secure page after checking the vendor and PO. No Google account is needed.

ELUXIOR Photo Intake

Send your PO photographs

Enter the PO number or follow the PO link in your Digest. The secure portal verifies your authorized vendor email and shows the current SKU list before any upload.

Enter a PO number to continue.

After email verification, check the PO, vendor and verified Style Numbers. Select a listed SKU and upload one photograph at a time. Additional photos may show retail packaging and branding, price tickets, master cartons, pallets, or paper markings on cartons. If an SKU is missing or cancelled, report the change to ELUXIOR for reconciliation with the PO.

“Upload confirmed” means that the file was received for that PO and SKU. The vendor is responsible for photographing the correct ordered product. Keep the receipt ID and retry failed files only.

Vendor Guidelines

Verify the SKU count and Style Numbers against the latest PO. If an SKU was added or cancelled, contact ELUXIOR so the authoritative PO list can be corrected. The current PO and official TJX instructions take precedence for packing, compliance and shipping.
