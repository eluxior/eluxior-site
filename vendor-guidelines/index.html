ELUXIOR Vendor Guidelines & Buyer Meeting Preparation

This page is your operational reference for preparing TJX buyer meetings, documenting Purchase Order products and using the ELUXIOR AI support chat. Always follow your current PO, the latest official TJX instructions and written directions from ELUXIOR/TJX.

1. Product Photography — Every PO

Required for every new Purchase Order: provide at least one clear photograph of each distinct SKU, even if no SKU Card was created during the buyer meeting.

  • Use the PO Photo Sheet supplied by ELUXIOR, or the individual SKU Cards when provided.
  • Place the identification sheet beside the product. Show the PO number and write the SKU / Style Reference clearly.
  • Photograph the entire product with sufficient lighting; add close-ups where finish, details or defects need clarification.
  • A SKU repeated across several DCs of the same PO needs only one reference photograph; document different sizes, colours or finishes separately.
  • Keep production-sample photographs and record any differences from the approved reference.
  • Follow the upload or submission instructions in your ELUXIOR Digest. Do not assume an emailed photo has been linked to your PO until confirmed.

Photo Cards with QR codes: PO-specific and SKU-specific download links will be provided in the Digest when available. Do not reuse a QR from another PO.

2. Before a Buyer Visits Your Showroom

  • Start preparing at least two weeks in advance. Review previous POs, buyer feedback, trend boards and relevant product developments.
  • Display past TJX orders in a separate area, labelled with Style Nr, PO number and past shipment/delivery date.
  • Organise new collections by colour/design and, where useful, display shapes and sizes separately.
  • Every product must have a stable Style Nr, complete dimensions, price, currency and realistic lead time before the buyer arrives.
  • Prepare stock opportunities separately, with a complete Excel offer showing pictures, dimensions, prices, available quantities and readiness.
  • Clean and organise the showroom; assign an assistant to handle products, photographs and structured notes.

3. During and After the Buyer Meeting

  • Focus the meeting on selections and decisions, not searching for prices or measuring products.
  • Record selected Style Nrs, changes, colours, sizes, target prices, quantities, sample requests and deadlines.
  • Clarify uncertain points immediately; with the buyer and ELUXIOR present, record a short recap video when appropriate.
  • Before the buyer leaves, confirm selections, actions, owners, deadlines and unresolved questions.
  • Send a verified written recap promptly and follow up on quotations and developments without repeated reminders.
  • Prepare requested samples quickly and correctly, but never ship TJX samples before ELUXIOR/TJX approval and confirmed shipping instructions.
  • Offer water and basic refreshments; do not offer gifts or lunch/dinner invitations.

4. At Trade Fairs — Ambiente, Maison & Objet and Others

  • Prepare every displayed SKU with dimensions, weight, prices and lead time; keep photos or PDF sheets for variants not displayed.
  • Show branding examples, export packing/pallet photographs and a short company video (15 seconds ideal; under 30 seconds).
  • Identify each buyer by full name, company and division; request a business card and start a separate notebook page for every customer.
  • Photograph seriously discussed products with the buyer's business card beside them, when appropriate, and keep each buyer's material separate.
  • Record notes and short video clips, confirm action owners and deadlines, and photograph the notebook pages.
  • Send selection photos, notes and clips to your team immediately; share TJX meeting material with ELUXIOR while the fair is in progress.
  • Acknowledge buyer emails within four working hours whenever reasonably possible. Be commercially flexible on initial MOQ where sustainable.

5. Packing — Store Ready & Nest Code

Always use the Store Ready and Nest Code configuration shown in the official PO and Distribution Instructions. Confirm any discrepancy with ELUXIOR before packing.

  • Regular Packing: one SKU per carton; a Store Ready Nest Code is not required.
  • Store Ready Assorted: different SKUs form a defined assortment and share one Nest Code; they do not need to fit physically inside one another.
  • Store Ready Nested: different SKUs physically fit inside one another and share one Nest Code.
  • Record units of each SKU per assortment separately from the number of assortments per master carton.
  • Check carton dimensions, weights, inner/master cartons, distribution by DC and all applicable marking instructions.

These explanations are a quick guide only. The official PO, Distro and current ELUXIOR Vendor Procedures Handbook take precedence.
